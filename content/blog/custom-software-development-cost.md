## Why there is no single price

Asking "how much does software cost" is like asking how much a building costs. A two-screen internal tool and a multi-tenant SaaS platform are different projects. What matters is understanding the cost drivers.

## The main cost drivers

### 1. Scope and complexity
The number of workflows, screens and business rules is the biggest driver. Complex calculations — such as payroll or pricing engines — take more effort than simple forms.

### 2. User roles and permissions
Each role (employee, manager, admin, customer) adds screens, permissions and testing.

### 3. Integrations
Payment gateways, accounting tools, WhatsApp, government APIs or legacy databases each require design, development and testing.

### 4. Platforms
Web only, or web plus Android and iOS apps? Cross-platform frameworks reduce but do not eliminate the extra effort.

### 5. Data migration
Cleaning and importing existing data is often underestimated.

### 6. Security and compliance
Audit logs, encryption, data retention and access reviews add effort — and are worth it.

### 7. Support and hosting
Budget for hosting, monitoring and maintenance after launch.

## How to control cost

- **Start with discovery.** A short paid discovery produces a clear scope and reliable estimate.
- **Define an MVP.** Build the smallest version that delivers real value.
- **Phase the roadmap.** Add modules once the first release proves itself.
