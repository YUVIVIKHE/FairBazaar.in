## What an ERP does

An ERP connects inventory, purchasing, sales, finance and operations in one database so every department works from the same numbers.

## Ready-made ERP: pros and cons

**Pros:** proven modules, faster start for standard processes.
**Cons:** heavy customisation costs, rigid workflows, long implementations.

## Custom or modular ERP: pros and cons

**Pros:** fits your operations, phased rollout, integrates with existing tools.
**Cons:** requires a capable development partner and good discovery.

## A phased approach

1. Inventory and item master
2. Sales and billing
3. Purchasing
4. Production or operations
5. Finance integration and dashboards
