## Assessment
- Inventory applications, databases and dependencies
- Identify compliance and data residency needs

## Strategy
- Rehost, re-platform or refactor per application

## Architecture & security
- Network isolation, IAM, encryption, secrets management

## Data
- Migration plan, validation and backups

## Delivery
- CI/CD, infrastructure as code, monitoring and alerts

## Testing & cutover
- Performance tests, rollback plan, communication plan

## Operate
- Cost reviews, patching, disaster recovery drills
