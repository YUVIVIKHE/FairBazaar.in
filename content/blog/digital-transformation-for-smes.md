## Why SMEs benefit most

In an SME, one connected system can replace several manual processes, and the owner feels the difference immediately.

## Where to start

- **Sales follow-ups** → [CRM](/products/crm)
- **Stock and billing** → [ERP / Inventory](/products/erp)
- **Attendance and payroll** → [HRMS](/products/hrms)

## A phased plan

1. Map processes and pain points
2. Pick the first process
3. Implement and train
4. Measure
5. Connect the next module

## Driving adoption

Involve the people who do the work, keep the interface simple and make the new system the only way to complete the task.
