## Step 1: Find the right process
Look for high volume, clear rules and measurable delay or error.

## Step 2: Map the current workflow
Document every step, handoff and system involved.

## Step 3: Simplify before automating
Automating a bad process makes it fail faster.

## Step 4: Define triggers, conditions and actions
For example: *when* a purchase request exceeds a threshold, *then* route to finance approval and notify the requester.

## Step 5: Connect systems
Use APIs and webhooks to connect CRM, ERP, HRMS and messaging.

## Step 6: Test and launch
Run real scenarios, including exceptions.

## Step 7: Measure and expand
Track cycle time and errors; move to the next process.

## Adding AI
When inputs are emails or documents, AI can classify and extract data first. Read [how AI automates business operations](/blog/ai-business-automation).
