## Why the choice matters

Your software partner shapes how your business will operate for years. A good partner reduces risk; a poor one creates a system nobody wants to maintain.

## The checklist

### Discovery and understanding
- Do they ask about your business before talking about technology?
- Do they offer a structured discovery phase?

### Technical depth
- Have they built similar systems — CRM, ERP, HRMS, AI?
- Can they explain their architecture choices in plain language?

### Communication
- Will you see working software every sprint?
- Is there a named point of contact?

### Ownership and transparency
- Will you have access to the code repository?
- Who owns the code and data?

### Quality and security
- Automated tests? Code reviews? Security practices?
- How are credentials and personal data protected?

### Support
- What happens after launch? Response times? Maintenance plans?

## Red flags

- A fixed quote after a single call.
- No written scope or change-request process.
- Reluctance to share code access.
