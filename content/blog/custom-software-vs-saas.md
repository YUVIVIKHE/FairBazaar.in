## The build-vs-buy question

Every growing business eventually asks: should we subscribe to another tool or build something of our own? The answer is rarely all-or-nothing. It depends on each process.

## Where SaaS wins

- **Standard processes.** Email, basic accounting and video meetings rarely need customisation.
- **Speed.** You can be productive the same day.
- **Low upfront cost.** Monthly subscriptions avoid capital investment.

## Where custom software wins

- **Differentiating workflows.** Your pricing logic, dealer schemes or service delivery model.
- **Integration depth.** When data must flow between many systems reliably.
- **Total cost at scale.** Per-user fees across hundreds of users add up.
- **Ownership and control.** Your roadmap, your data model, your hosting.

## A simple decision framework

Ask four questions for each process:

1. Is this process a source of competitive advantage?
2. Does a SaaS tool cover at least 80% of it without workarounds?
3. How many users will need access in three years?
4. How many other systems must it exchange data with?

If the answers are "no, yes, few, few" — buy SaaS. If they lean "yes, no, many, many" — consider custom or a configurable platform.
