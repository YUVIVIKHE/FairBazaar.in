## Why organisations adopt HRMS

As teams grow, HR work multiplies: attendance registers, leave balances, salary calculations, expense claims and documents. An HRMS brings it into one system.

## Core HRMS modules

- **Employee management** — profiles, departments, reporting lines.
- **Attendance** — including [location-based attendance](/blog/location-based-attendance-guide) for field teams.
- **Leave and holidays** — policies, balances and approvals.
- **Payroll** — attendance-linked salary calculation and payslips.
- **Expenses and invoices** — claims with receipts and approvals.
- **Performance and work progress** — goals, reviews and daily logs.
- **Documents** — secure employee document storage.
- **Reports and analytics** — headcount, attrition, costs.

## Benefits

- Accurate attendance and payroll
- Faster approvals
- Employee self-service
- Better workforce decisions
