## Signs your CRM doesn't fit

- Salespeople keep leads in personal notebooks or spreadsheets.
- Custom fields and workarounds everywhere.
- Reports require exporting to Excel.
- Dealers or partners can't be managed properly.

## Comparison

| | Ready-made CRM | Custom / configurable CRM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup time | Days | Weeks |
| Fit | Generic | Your process |
| Pricing | Per user, ongoing | Build + hosting |
| Integrations | Available connectors | Anything with an API |
