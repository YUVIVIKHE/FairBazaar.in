This SLA framework describes availability and support commitments for FairBazaar SaaS products. The specific service levels that apply to you — including any service credits — are those stated in your order form or subscription agreement.

## 1. Availability
We design our SaaS products for high availability with monitoring, automated backups and incident response procedures. The availability target for your plan is stated in your agreement and is measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and events outside our reasonable control.

## 2. Scheduled maintenance
Planned maintenance is performed during low-usage hours (Indian Standard Time) where possible, with advance notice for maintenance expected to cause noticeable disruption.

## 3. Support priorities
| Priority | Description | Target first response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 – Critical | Service unavailable or data at risk | As defined in your plan |
| P2 – High | Major feature impaired, no workaround | As defined in your plan |
| P3 – Normal | Partial impairment or question | As defined in your plan |
| P4 – Low | Feature request or cosmetic issue | As defined in your plan |

## 4. Incident communication
For major incidents we notify affected customers, provide updates until resolution and share a summary of root cause and remediation.

## 5. Exclusions
Issues caused by customer-side systems, misuse contrary to the [Acceptable Use Policy](/acceptable-use-policy), third-party services outside our control, or force majeure events are excluded.
