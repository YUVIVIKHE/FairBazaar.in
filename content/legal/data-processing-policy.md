This policy describes how FairBazaar processes personal data on behalf of customers ("Customers") who use FairBazaar SaaS products or platforms we operate for them. A Customer-specific Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") may be signed and prevails over this policy.

## 1. Roles
The Customer determines the purpose and means of processing its data (for example, employee records in FairBazaar HRMS) and acts as the data fiduciary/controller. FairBazaar processes that data only on the Customer's documented instructions as a data processor.

## 2. Scope of processing
- **Categories of data:** as configured by the Customer — for example employee identifiers, attendance and location at check-in, leave, payroll inputs, expense receipts, and customer or lead records.
- **Purpose:** providing, securing, supporting and maintaining the service.

## 3. Our commitments
- Process personal data only on documented instructions.
- Ensure personnel with access are bound by confidentiality.
- Apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures (see [Security](/security)).
- Engage sub-processors only under written obligations at least as protective as this policy, and inform Customers of material changes.
- Assist Customers in responding to data principal requests and in meeting their obligations under the DPDP Act, 2023.
- Notify Customers without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting their data.
- At the end of the service, return or delete Customer data as instructed, subject to legal retention requirements.

## 4. Location-based attendance
Location data in FairBazaar HRMS is captured at check-in/check-out events for verification against configured geo-fences. Customers are responsible for informing employees and obtaining any required consent.

## 5. Contact
Customers can request a DPA or information about sub-processors via the [contact page]({{contactPath}}).
