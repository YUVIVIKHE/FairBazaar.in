FairBazaar wants everyone to be able to use this Website, including people with disabilities.

## Our approach
We design and build with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA in mind:
- Semantic HTML with a logical heading structure and landmarks.
- Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators and a "Skip to content" link.
- Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative visuals are hidden from assistive technology.
- Sufficient colour contrast for text and interface elements.
- Respect for the **prefers-reduced-motion** setting — animations are minimised when requested.
- Accessible forms with labels, error messages and status announcements.
- Captions for product videos.

## Known limitations
Some interactive visualisations convey information that is also provided in adjacent text. We continue to test with assistive technologies and improve.

## Feedback
If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please tell us through the [contact page]({{contactPath}}). We aim to respond and provide the information in an accessible format.
