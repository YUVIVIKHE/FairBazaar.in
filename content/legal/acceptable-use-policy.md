This Acceptable Use Policy applies to all users of FairBazaar SaaS products and platforms we operate ("Services").

## You must not
- Use the Services for any unlawful purpose or in violation of Indian or other applicable law.
- Upload content that infringes intellectual property or privacy rights, or that is defamatory, obscene or harmful.
- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to the Services, other accounts or underlying infrastructure.
- Interfere with or disrupt the Services, including by introducing malware, excessive automated requests or load testing without permission.
- Use AI features to generate unlawful, deceptive or harmful content, or to impersonate others.
- Collect personal data of others through the Services without a lawful basis.
- Resell or sublicense the Services without written permission.

## Enforcement
We may suspend or restrict access where we reasonably believe this policy has been breached, and will notify the account owner where appropriate.
