This Cookie Policy explains how {{url}} uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage.

## 1. Categories we use

### Essential (always on)
Required for the Website to function and remain secure — for example remembering your cookie choices (`fb-consent-v1`) and storing the landing page and campaign source for an enquiry you submit (`fb-attribution`, session storage only).

### Analytics (optional)
With your consent we may use Google Analytics and/or Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the Website so we can improve it. These tools are not loaded until you opt in.

### Marketing (optional)
With your consent we may use the Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns. It is not loaded until you opt in.

## 2. Managing your choices
You can accept all, reject non-essential, or customise your choices in the banner shown on your first visit. You can change your choices at any time using **Cookie settings** in the website footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

## 3. Changes
We will update this policy if the technologies we use change.
