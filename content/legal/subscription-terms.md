## 1. Plans and users

## 2. Billing and taxes
Fees are billed in advance for the billing period in your order form. Prices are exclusive of GST and other applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

## 3. Renewal
Subscriptions renew automatically for the same period unless either party gives notice before the end of the current term as specified in the order form.

## 5. Data export
You can request an export of your data in a commonly used format during your subscription and for a limited period after it ends, as specified in your agreement. After that period, data is deleted from active systems subject to backup cycles and legal requirements.
